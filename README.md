# EcoMind-Sustainable-Living-Recommender
EcoMind is a Responsible AI-powered sustainable living recommender designed to help users adopt eco-friendly habits through personalized, data-driven insights. The product addresses a key gap where users want to live sustainably but struggle due to generic advice, lack of measurable impact, and low engagement. EcoMind solves this by delivering context-aware recommendations based on user behavior, lifestyle, and environmental data.

The platform targets urban professionals, students, and sustainability-conscious users. Research highlights major pain points such as lack of personalization, absence of impact tracking, and low motivation. Based on these insights, EcoMind offers core features including a personalized recommendation engine, impact tracker (CO₂ and water savings), adaptive dashboard, AI transparency panel, and optional gamification to improve engagement.

The product follows an MVP-first, Agile development approach. Its AI system uses user inputs to generate sustainability scores and recommendations, continuously improving through feedback loops. A strong data strategy ensures privacy, transparency, and fairness while integrating external datasets like carbon footprint metrics and weather data.

Performance is measured through KPIs such as engagement (>60%), adoption (>40%), accuracy (>75%), satisfaction (>80%), and retention (>50%). AI evaluation frameworks (goldens, auto-raters, LLM-as-judge) ensure reliability, achieving 85% relevance and 90% fairness.

User testing shows high usability, with 90% task completion and 4.4/5 satisfaction. Key improvements include simplifying explanations and enhancing visualization. Continuous iteration through Agile sprints ensures product refinement.
